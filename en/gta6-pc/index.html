How to tell if a GTA 6 for PC is fake.

On November 23 the Xbox executable leaked, and about thirty projects are already trying to run it on PC. None of them is playable yet. Anything offered to you to download and play today is fake.

None of them has an installer or a game to download. Their pages only have code for developers, and they ask you to bring your own copy of the game. If someone sends you a “ready GTA 6 PC” in the name of any of these projects, it's a lie.

Signs of a fake

If you see even one of these, close it.

There's an .exe or an “installer” to download.The real projects don't have that.
They ask for money, crypto or a “donation”.People are already using the projects' names to sell coins.
They send you to Telegram or a shortened link.The originals are on GitHub.
The name is almost the same as the real one.One letter changed, or “official”, “full” or “crack” added.
Thousands of stars, zero changes.A new account with no real code changes: stars can be bought.
It asks you to turn off your antivirus.Or to “run as administrator” to work.
If you downloaded something

What to do right now, in this order.

01Don't open it again

If you already opened it, disconnect the PC from the internet.

02Check it on VirusTotal

Upload it to virustotal.com. If even one engine flags it, delete it and empty the Recycle Bin.

03Scan with Windows

Windows Security, Virus & threat protection, Scan options, “Microsoft Defender Offline scan”.

04Change your passwords

From another device, not that PC: email, Steam, Rockstar, Discord and your bank.

05Sign out of open sessions

On every account (in Google: Security, Your devices) and turn on two-step verification.

If you absolutely want to try something: open it inside Windows Sandbox (Windows 10/11 Pro, turn it on under “Windows Features”). Everything that happens there is wiped when you close it.
