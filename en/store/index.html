Store owner guide · Black Friday 2026

Stop scammers from using your name on your customers.

This week, fake links copying real stores are spreading on WhatsApp: same logo, same photos, impossible prices. Your customer pays, nothing arrives, and the complaint lands on you.

Steps4
Time20 min
Cost0
01
Today

Warn them before the fake link reaches them.

You can't stop people from copying your brand. You can make sure your customers recognize the copy before they pay. Copy the message, change the highlighted parts and send it.

Broadcast list · WhatsApp
Hi. This week fake links are going around using the name [your store] with prices that aren't real. Our only official channels are: Website: [yoursite.com] WhatsApp: [this number] Instagram: [@yourinstagram] We will never ask you to pay by transfer to a personal account or with gift cards. If you get a deal that looks too good, message us here before paying and we'll confirm within minutes.

Email or website banner
This Black Friday we only sell on [yoursite.com]. If you see our deals on any other link, don't pay and message us.
02
This week

Let your customer verify you in ten seconds.

  • One single official link, the same in your Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp bio.
  • A complete WhatsApp Business profile: name, logo, address, website and hours.
  • Payment methods in writing on your website or catalog: “We only accept payments via…”.
  • Same name and same photo on every network. Copies change one letter or add “deals”, “official” or “outlet”.
03
If a copy shows up

Report it and warn again.

  1. Keep evidence: a screenshot of the page, the full link and the message it arrived in, with the date.
  2. Google: search “Google Safe Browsing report phishing” and paste the link, so Chrome shows the red warning.
  3. Instagram and Facebook: on the profile or ad, three-dot menu, Report, “Pretending to be someone else or a business”.
  4. WhatsApp: open the chat, tap the contact's name, Report.
  5. Warn your customers again with a crossed-out screenshot of the copy, so they recognize it.
If someone already paid: they should call their bank that same day to dispute the charge and file a report with their country's cybercrime unit.
